# Endings/routes

## Common ending
You write a great research paper. Your bet(t)a fish loves you. But let's talk about the research paper.
## Bad endings

In general the bad endings are meant to be jokes that do not punish or scare the player. Always allow them to be recoverable.
- Saying "ew a bug!" when you fish a shrimp up (maybe not an ending, but a flag)
- Eating a shrimp and discovering you have a shellfish intolerance
