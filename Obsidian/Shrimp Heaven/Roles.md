# Project Manager
Requirements: organizational skills, the willingness to lead, proficiency with Obsidian preferred

Responsibilities:
- Maintain and delegate a public task list within Obsidian and Git as part of a project plan
- Create and (gently) enforce timelines, leading team meetings at regular intervals to check on team progress, and pivot when necessary
- Be the contact point for team members to report blockers, delays, or other issues
- Always make decisions with the entire team's input, either via team meeting or Discord polls as necessary
# Coder
Requirements: proficiency in (or eagerness to learn) Python, Ren'Py, and Git

Responsibilities:
- Easy: Translate plain text to Ren'Py format
- Easy: Set sound effects, background music, and sprite info to activate at the proper moments within the story
- Intermediate: Design basic "logic gates" to choose between branching storylines
- Intermediate: Manage save data
- Advanced: Build out fishing minigame

If you'd like to learn intermediate-to-advanced Python/general coding principles to prepare you for this role, I recommend you try playing [The Farmer Was Replaced](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2060160/The_Farmer_Was_Replaced/).
# Artist
Requirements: communication skills, ability to use software that inputs/outputs our agreed-upon file format for ease of sharing raw files

Responsibilities:
- Your choice(s) of creative pipeline assistance: concept art, sketches, lineart, colors, rendering/final color balance
- Adhere to a general style guide for consistency across assets
- Stay in communication with writers and fellow artists to collaborate on assets
# Writer
Requirements: willingness to work with Git, Obsidian, and possibly Python; the grace to accept that some concepts must be discarded

Responsibilities:
- Idea generation, outlining, and writing of scenes for one or more shrimp dating storylines
- Work primarily independently on developing storylines for your designated shrimp love interest, while soliciting and accepting the feedback of fellow collaborators
- Write natural, mostly lighthearted dialogue for your shrimp(s) while sticking to the general characterization of the MC
- Advanced: Write dialogue, sprite changes, and music/sound cues directly into Ren'Py
# Editor
Requirements: basic Ren'Py fluency, confidence in grammar

Responsibilities:
- Design and adhere to character and style guides to alter writing for consistent tone, grammar, and characterization across scenes and routes
- Read through and make direct edits to text embedded within source code
# Musician
Requirements: i will take Anything

Responsibilities:
- Create high-quality loopable recordings or mixes of original or public domain music to suit the moods of various story beats
# Playtester
Requirements: attention to detail

Responsibilities:
- Download development builds of the game to play through with the explicit goal to "break" the program
- Open bug reports within GitHub and document exact steps to recreate the error with as much detail as possible
- Provide basic but specific proofreading/critical feedback on the overall gameplay and story
# Voice Actor
Requirements: confidence and the ability to record clean, professional audio

Responsibilities:
- "Audition" for your character(s) of choice with a quick demo of the voice & direction you're thinking of
- Take direction from the team for ways to tweak your performance if applicable
- Record key lines and generic cut-ins for character(s) selected
# Helper
Requirements: an eagerness to do any of the above

Responsibilities:
- Respond to pings of the helper role in Discord if you can help with a task
